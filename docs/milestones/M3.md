## 1. What is Live in Production

- **Production URLs:** [https://life-rony.vercel.app](https://life-rony.vercel.app) & [https://life.rhrony05.me](https://life.rhrony05.me)
- **Database & Integrity:**
  - Tables utilized: `public.trackers`, `public.tracker_items`, `public.expenses`, `public.people`, `public.money_entries`, `public.owner_settings`.
  - Zero disruption: All Vocab tables (`words`, `quiz_attempts`) remain completely untouched.
- **Pages & Features:**
  - **/trackers (V4):**
    - **Bulk Paste Importer:** Accepts structured markdown (headings for groups `## Group`, bullets `- Item`, optional markdown links `[Title](url)`) and provides live parse preview counts.
    - **Progress & Celebration:** Formula `done / total * 100`, progress bars per card and detail pane, and celebration banner on 100% completion.
    - **Two-Way Today Sync:** Click "Add to today" to schedule an item onto the Today board with a `[Tracker Title]` chip. Marking the task complete on the Today board updates the tracker item, and vice versa.
    - **Today Rail Block:** "Trackers" rail section displaying all active trackers with live progress percentages.
    - **Desktop & Mobile Parity:** Two-pane master-detail view on desktop (≥1024px) with `j`/`k`/`x` keyboard shortcuts, and responsive single-pane drilldown on mobile with back button.
  - **/money (V5):**
    - **Tab 1: Spending:**
      - **SpendTiles:** Today, This week (Saturday → Friday in Asia/Dhaka), and This month tiles.
      - **BudgetBar:** Visual monthly budget bar with dynamic states (`ok`, `warn` at ≥80%, `over` at >100%), showing exact remaining or over-budget amounts in whole BDT taka (`৳12,34,567`).
      - **QuickSpend Form:** Fast keyboard-first logging (Amount → Enter). 8 categories (`food`, `transport`, `bills`, `groceries`, `tech`, `health`, `entertainment`, `other`), auto-remembers last used category, optional note and date.
      - **CategoryBars:** Visual monthly breakdown showing category icons, total amounts, and percentages.
      - **SpendList:** Expenses grouped by day, inline edit dialog, and delete with confirmation.
    - **Tab 2: IOUs (Money):**
      - **MoneyTotals:** Three overview summary tiles: "Others owe you", "You owe", and "Net" balance.
      - **EntryDialog:** Add IOU modal with person autocomplete, 4-way transaction kind (`lent`, `borrowed`, `repaid`, `received`), ৳ amount, date, and note. Press `a` from anywhere on the tab to open.
      - **PeopleList:** Shows each person with human-friendly balance sentences (e.g., *"Fahim owes you ৳500"* or *"You owe Karim ৳200"*), quick **Settle** button with confirmation dialog, and a collapsed **Settled** divider for zero-balance contacts.
      - **HistoryPane:** Complete transaction ledger for each person with directional arrow indicators, person menu (rename, delete), and delete entry confirmation with automatic balance adjustment.
    - **Today Rail Block:**
      - Integrated **Money** block showing Today's spend, monthly budget progress bar, and top IOU balances.

### How to Use It in 4 Lines:
1. Go to **/trackers** (or `g` `t`): Paste your syllabus or NeetCode 150 list, click **Add to today** on an item to schedule it to your board with automatic two-way sync.
2. Go to **/money** (or `g` `m`): Type an amount (e.g. `120`) and press **Enter** to instantly log your lunch spend under Food.
3. Click **Set budget** to establish your monthly spending ceiling and watch the visual budget bar update in real-time.
4. Switch to the **IOUs** tab: Press `a` to log a debt with a friend; click **Settle** once they pay back to cleanly archive them into the Settled section.

## 3. Code-Flow Walkthrough: QuickSpend to Budget & Rail

Here is the exact flow when Rony logs a daily spend:

```
[Browser UI: QuickSpend.tsx]
   │ 1. Rony types "120" and hits Enter (category defaults to "Food" or last-used).
   ▼
[POST /api/spending (src/app/api/spending/route.ts)]
   │ 2. `requireOwner()` validates owner session.
   │ 3. Payload validated by `CreateExpenseSchema` (amount: 120, category: 'food', spent_on: YYYY-MM-DD).
   │ 4. `createExpense()` (src/lib/services/spending.ts):
   │    - Inserts row into `public.expenses` with whole BDT amount.
   │    - Returns ExpenseDto (status 201).
   ▼
[Browser State Refetch]
   │ 5. `MoneyClient.tsx` re-fetches `/api/spending/summary` and `/api/spending`.
   │ 6. `summariseSpending()` (src/lib/domain/spending.ts):
   │    - Calculates Today's total (+120).
   │    - Recalculates Saturday-to-Friday week total using `weekRange(now)`.
   │    - Recalculates month total and updates `budgetState(spentThisMonth, monthlyBudget)`.
   │    - Recalculates Food category share: `round(foodTotal / monthTotal * 100)`.
   ▼
[UI Updates: SpendTiles, BudgetBar, CategoryBars]
   │ 7. Today tile increments by ৳120.
   │ 8. BudgetBar fills with new percentage and shows remaining budget (e.g. "৳7,270 left").
   │ 9. CategoryBars shows Food bar at updated percentage.
   ▼
[Today Board Rail Update]
   │ 10. Navigating back to Today board reflects the new spend under the "Money" rail block
   │     (Spent today: ৳120, month budget progress bar filled).
```
